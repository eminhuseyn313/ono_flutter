# [HEALTH] Expert Domain: Fitness & Health

## Purpose
Ensure all Body lane content in ONO is safe, evidence-based, and appropriately scaled for young men aged 18-30 with varying fitness levels. Cover workout programming, nutrition fundamentals, recovery protocols, and injury prevention. The app must never replace medical advice but should provide a credible foundation for physical development.

## Scope Within ONO
- **Body Lane**: All workout content, progression models, and fitness challenges
- **Nutrition Content**: Micro-lessons on fueling, hydration, and meal planning
- **Recovery Protocols**: Sleep hygiene, rest day programming, active recovery
- **Workout Verification**: Photo/AI-based exercise completion validation
- **Apprentice Mode Body Track**: Beginner-safe introductory fitness programming

## Quality Standards
1. All exercise programming must follow ACSM or NSCA guidelines for the target demographic
2. Progressive overload must be systematic — never random intensity spikes
3. Nutrition content must avoid supplement promotion, extreme diets, or body shaming
4. Every workout must include warm-up and cool-down components
5. Exercise descriptions must include form cues, common mistakes, and modification options
6. Caloric/macro guidance must account for varied body types without promoting obsessive tracking
7. No content should require gym access — bodyweight alternatives for every movement

## Review Checklist
- [ ] Does the workout program follow a logical periodization model (linear, undulating, or block)?
- [ ] Are rest periods specified between sets and between training days?
- [ ] Does nutrition content avoid absolute claims ("you must eat X")?
- [ ] Are injury risk exercises flagged with prerequisite strength/mobility benchmarks?
- [ ] Does the AI workout verification distinguish between exercise completion and proper form?
- [ ] Are deload weeks programmed every 4-6 weeks?
- [ ] Does content acknowledge individual variation (ectomorph/mesomorph framing is outdated — avoid)?
- [ ] Is recovery treated as equally important to training, not as optional?

## Content Guidelines
### Workout Programming
- **Beginner (Apprentice Mode)**: 3 days/week, full-body, bodyweight + light resistance
- **Intermediate**: 4 days/week, upper/lower or push/pull split
- **Advanced**: 5-6 days/week, specific programming based on user goals
- Every program must include: mobility work, compound movements, isolation accessories, cooldown
- RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) scale preferred over fixed weight prescriptions

### Progressive Overload Model
- Week 1-2: Learn movement patterns at low intensity
- Week 3-4: Increase volume (sets/reps)
- Week 5-6: Increase intensity (load/difficulty)
- Week 7: Deload (reduce volume by 40-50%)
- Repeat cycle with new baseline

### Nutrition Basics
- Frame as "fueling performance" not "fixing your body"
- Teach principles: protein sufficiency, hydration, meal timing flexibility
- Avoid: calorie counting for beginners, cheat meal/clean eating language, supplement stacks
- Include: budget-friendly meal ideas, cooking skill micro-lessons, grocery list templates

### Recovery Protocols
- Sleep: Educate on 7-9 hour target, sleep hygiene habits, screen time impact
- Active recovery: Walking, stretching, yoga flows on rest days
- Stress management: Connect to Mind lane — recovery is mental + physical

### Injury Prevention
- Movement screening questions during onboarding
- "Pain vs. discomfort" education
- Clear escalation: "If sharp pain occurs, stop and consult a healthcare provider"
- Joint mobility prerequisites before heavy compound lifts

## Red Flags to Avoid
- **Bro science**: Unverified claims about supplements, meal timing myths, "no pain no gain"
- **Body dysmorphia triggers**: Before/after comparisons, ideal body type imagery, body fat % obsession
- **Overtraining glorification**: "Rest is for the weak" messaging
- **Extreme diets**: Keto/carnivore/fasting promotion without medical context
- **Unqualified medical advice**: The app must never diagnose injuries or prescribe treatment
- **Performance-enhancing substance normalization**: Zero tolerance for PED-adjacent content
