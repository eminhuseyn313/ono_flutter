# [PSYCH] Expert Domain: Behavioral Psychology

## Purpose
Govern all psychological frameworks within ONO — habit formation loops, emotional arc design, notification psychology, reflection prompts, and the Apprentice Mode onboarding system. Ensure the app motivates without manipulating, challenges without shaming, and builds genuine self-efficacy in young men aged 18-30.

## Scope Within ONO
- **All 4 Lanes**: Emotional arc across Mind, Body, Social, Skill progression
- **Apprentice Mode**: First 21-day onboarding experience and validation criteria
- **Notification System**: Timing, tone, frequency, and escalation psychology
- **Reflection Prompts**: Daily/weekly self-assessment question design
- **Streak & Reward Mechanics**: Healthy motivation vs. compulsive engagement

## Quality Standards
1. Every behavioral nudge must have a cited psychological basis (CBT, ACT, SDT, or peer-reviewed habit research)
2. No dark patterns — no artificial urgency, guilt-based retention, or loss-aversion exploitation
3. All-or-nothing thinking must be actively countered (e.g., "missed a day" messaging must normalize imperfection)
4. Reflection prompts must be open-ended, never leading toward a single "correct" emotional state
5. Notification copy must pass the "would a therapist say this?" test
6. Progress framing must emphasize trajectory over absolute position

## Review Checklist
- [ ] Does this feature use intrinsic motivation (autonomy, competence, relatedness) over extrinsic rewards?
- [ ] Could a user with anxiety or depression interpret this message negatively?
- [ ] Does the streak mechanic include a grace period or "partial credit" system?
- [ ] Are reflection prompts validated against rumination risk?
- [ ] Does Apprentice Mode exit criteria measure genuine engagement, not just time elapsed?
- [ ] Is notification frequency capped per day with user-adjustable controls?
- [ ] Does failure messaging include a recovery path, not just acknowledgment?
- [ ] Are emotional arcs designed with both peaks and intentional rest periods?

## Content Guidelines
### Habit Formation
- Use Fogg Behavior Model: Motivation + Ability + Prompt = Behavior
- Anchor new habits to existing routines (implementation intentions)
- Start with "tiny habits" — 2-minute versions of target behaviors
- Celebrate immediately after completion, not at end-of-day

### Emotional Arc Design
- Weekly arcs: Monday activation → Wednesday challenge peak → Friday reflection → Weekend integration
- Never stack high-difficulty tasks across all 4 lanes on the same day
- Include "deload" weeks every 4th week across all lanes

### Notification Psychology
- Morning: Forward-looking, possibility-framed ("Today you could...")
- Midday: Social proof or accountability ("3 others in your cohort completed...")
- Evening: Reflection, never guilt ("How did today feel?" not "You didn't finish...")
- Maximum 4 notifications per day, user-configurable down to 0

### All-or-Nothing Prevention
- Replace binary completion (done/not done) with spectrum tracking (0-100%)
- "Partial wins count" messaging on every incomplete task
- Weekly summaries emphasize best day, not worst day
- Streak freezes available without penalty framing

## Red Flags to Avoid
- **Shame-based motivation**: "Real men don't skip days" or any gendered pressure
- **Toxic comparison**: Leaderboards that expose failure or rank users publicly
- **Addiction mechanics**: Infinite scroll, variable-ratio reward schedules, FOMO triggers
- **Pathologizing normal behavior**: Treating a missed day as a "relapse"
- **Unsolicited mental health diagnoses**: The app observes, never diagnoses
- **Guru positioning**: ONO is a tool, not a therapist or life coach
