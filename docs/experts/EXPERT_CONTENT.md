# [CONTENT] Expert Domain: Content Strategy & Curation

## Purpose
Govern ONO's content pipeline — role model research, trending content curation, micro-course authoring, tone of voice, and content moderation. Ensure all content motivates without toxicity, represents diverse success paths, and stays fresh through systematic curation with a 7-day TTL for trending items.

## Scope Within ONO
- **Role Model Content**: Research methodology, selection criteria, story formatting
- **Trending Content**: 7-day TTL curation cycle, source validation, relevance scoring
- **Micro-Courses**: Writing standards for 5-10 minute lessons across all lanes
- **Tone of Voice**: Motivational language guidelines that avoid toxic masculinity
- **Content Moderation**: User-generated content policies, AI-assisted screening
- **Cross-Lane Content**: Ensuring cohesive narrative across Mind, Body, Social, Skill

## Quality Standards
1. Every role model featured must be vetted for recent controversy (last 12 months minimum)
2. Trending content must come from verified sources — no unattributed claims or viral misinformation
3. Micro-courses must be self-contained — complete learning in one sitting, no cliffhangers for engagement
4. Tone must be tested with target demographic — "motivational" must not mean "aggressive"
5. Content moderation must have sub-4-hour response time for flagged material
6. All content must be original or properly licensed — no repurposed social media posts without permission
7. Diversity: Role models must represent varied backgrounds, industries, and success definitions

## Review Checklist
- [ ] Does the role model represent a healthy, achievable path (not billionaire exceptionalism)?
- [ ] Is trending content sourced from credible outlets with publication dates verified?
- [ ] Does the micro-course have a clear learning objective stated upfront?
- [ ] Would a woman, non-binary person, or someone outside the target demo find the tone respectful if they read it?
- [ ] Is the 7-day TTL enforced — does stale trending content automatically cycle out?
- [ ] Are content moderation decisions documented with reasoning?
- [ ] Does user-generated content pass through automated screening before visibility?
- [ ] Is the content accessible (reading level, jargon, assumed knowledge)?

## Content Guidelines
### Role Model Research Methodology
- **Source Requirements**: At minimum 3 independent sources confirming key claims about the individual
- **Vetting Process**: Background check for legal issues, public controversies, harmful statements
- **Refresh Cycle**: Re-vet featured role models every 6 months
- **Selection Criteria**:
  - Demonstrable growth journey (not just end-state success)
  - Relatable starting point for target demographic
  - Represents at least one ONO lane authentically
  - No active promotion of harmful ideologies
- **Story Format**: Challenge → Decision → Process → Outcome → Lesson (never just "he made it")
- **Diversity Targets**: No more than 30% from any single industry; include non-Western perspectives

### Trending Content Curation
- **7-Day TTL**: Every trending item has a publish date and auto-expires after 7 days
- **Sources**: Pre-approved source list (peer-reviewed journals, established media, verified experts)
- **Relevance Score**: Must connect to at least one ONO lane with a clear "why this matters to you" framing
- **Format**: Headline + 2-sentence summary + source link + lane tag + "Discuss" prompt
- **Volume**: 3-5 new trending items per day, curated not aggregated
- **Exclusions**: Celebrity gossip, political content, rage-bait, unverified claims

### Micro-Course Writing Standards
- **Length**: 800-1200 words or 5-10 minute audio equivalent
- **Structure**: Hook (why this matters) → Core concept → Example → Action step → Reflection prompt
- **Reading Level**: Flesch-Kincaid Grade 8-10 (accessible without being condescending)
- **Actionability**: Every lesson must end with a concrete action the user can take today
- **Assessment**: 2-3 comprehension/reflection questions, not quizzes — learning, not testing
- **Versioning**: Date-stamped, reviewed annually for accuracy, marked outdated when superseded

### Tone of Voice
- **Do**: "You're building something real" / "Small steps compound" / "Today's version of you showed up"
- **Don't**: "Crush it" / "No excuses" / "Outwork everyone" / "Be a beast"
- **Principles**:
  - Encouraging over demanding
  - Process-focused over outcome-obsessed
  - Inclusive masculinity — strength includes vulnerability, asking for help, emotional awareness
  - First-person framing: "I'm working on..." not "A real man would..."
- **Voice Attributes**: Warm, direct, knowledgeable, humble, occasionally funny — like a slightly older friend who figured some things out
- **Avoid**: Military metaphors, combat language, dominance framing, "grindset" vocabulary

### Content Moderation
- **User-Generated Content (UGC)**: All UGC screened by AI toxicity filter before publication
- **Appeals Process**: Users can appeal moderation decisions; human review within 48 hours
- **Escalation Matrix**:
  - Spam/irrelevant → Auto-remove, no penalty
  - Mild toxicity → Warning + content edit suggestion
  - Hate speech/harassment → Immediate removal + 24-hour restriction
  - Threats/illegal content → Immediate removal + permanent ban + law enforcement if applicable
- **Moderator Wellness**: Human moderators limited to 4-hour shifts with mandatory breaks

## Red Flags to Avoid
- **Toxic masculinity tropes**: "Alpha/beta" framing, dominance hierarchies, emotional suppression
- **Hustle porn**: Glorifying overwork, sleep deprivation, or neglecting relationships for success
- **Celebrity worship**: Presenting billionaires/influencers as aspirational without critical context
- **Survivorship bias**: Only showing success stories without acknowledging privilege, luck, or systemic factors
- **Rage-bait curation**: Trending content selected for anger engagement rather than genuine value
- **Plagiarism**: Repurposing content without attribution or licensing
- **Stale content**: Trending items older than 7 days still showing as "trending"
