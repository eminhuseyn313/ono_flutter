# [SPIRITUAL] Expert Domain: Meditation & Mindfulness

## Purpose
Guide the Mind lane's contemplative and philosophical content — meditation curriculum, breathing exercises, stoic philosophy integration, and mindfulness progression. Ensure depth without dogma, accessibility without dilution, and genuine inner development practice for young men aged 18-30 who may have zero prior exposure to these disciplines.

## Scope Within ONO
- **Mind Lane**: Meditation sessions, breathwork routines, philosophical reflections
- **Stoic Philosophy**: Daily stoic principles integrated as practical decision-making tools
- **Breathing Exercises**: Structured breathwork progressions (box breathing, Wim Hof basics, 4-7-8)
- **Mindfulness Progression**: Beginner to advanced meditation curriculum over 90+ days
- **Cross-Lane Integration**: Mental preparation for Body challenges, emotional processing for Social interactions

## Quality Standards
1. All meditation instructions must be secular — no religious framing unless historically contextualizing a practice
2. Stoic philosophy must be sourced from primary texts (Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius, Seneca) not social media interpretations
3. Breathing exercises must include contraindications (e.g., Wim Hof not suitable for those with cardiovascular conditions)
4. Progression must be scaffolded — never jump from 2-minute to 20-minute sits without intermediate steps
5. Content must respect that mindfulness can surface difficult emotions — include grounding techniques
6. Avoid spiritual bypassing — "just meditate" is never the answer to systemic or clinical issues
7. Philosophy content must provoke thought, not prescribe beliefs

## Content Guidelines
### Meditation Curriculum
- **Week 1-2**: Breath awareness, 2-3 minutes, guided audio
- **Week 3-4**: Body scan, 5 minutes, semi-guided
- **Week 5-8**: Focused attention (candle, sound, breath), 7-10 minutes
- **Week 9-12**: Open monitoring, 10-15 minutes
- **Week 13+**: Self-directed practice with optional themes
- Always offer: sitting, walking, and movement-based meditation options

### Breathing Exercises
- **Foundation**: Diaphragmatic breathing (teach first, prerequisite for all others)
- **Calming**: 4-7-8 breathing, extended exhale techniques
- **Energizing**: Box breathing, Wim Hof method (with full safety protocol)
- **Integration**: Pre-workout breathwork, pre-sleep wind-down sequences
- Every session: duration, rep count, rest between rounds, when to stop

### Stoic Philosophy Integration
- Present as practical tools, not moral commandments
- Daily format: Quote + Context + "How does this apply today?" prompt
- Key themes: Dichotomy of control, memento mori (without nihilism), voluntary discomfort, journaling
- Avoid: "Alpha male" stoicism, emotional suppression disguised as "equanimity," hustle culture mapping
- Include: Stoic vulnerability (Marcus Aurelius wrote about fear and doubt openly)

### Mindfulness Progression Markers
- Beginner: Can sit for 5 minutes with guided support
- Developing: Notices mind-wandering and returns attention without self-judgment
- Intermediate: Practices 10+ minutes, begins applying mindfulness in daily situations
- Advanced: Self-directed practice, can use mindfulness during high-stress moments

## Red Flags to Avoid
- **Toxic stoicism**: "Real men don't feel emotions" repackaged as philosophy
- **Spiritual bypassing**: Using mindfulness to avoid addressing real problems
- **Cultural appropriation**: Stripping practices from their traditions without acknowledgment
- **Pseudoscience claims**: "Meditation rewires your DNA" or unverified neuroscience claims
- **Guru culture**: Positioning any teacher or the app as an enlightenment authority
- **Forced positivity**: "Just be grateful" as a response to genuine suffering
- **Hyperventilation risk**: Breathwork without proper safety protocols and contraindications
