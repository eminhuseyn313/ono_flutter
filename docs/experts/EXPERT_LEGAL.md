# [LEGAL] Expert Domain: Legal & Compliance

## Purpose
Address all legal considerations for ONO — celebrity likeness and right of publicity, GDPR compliance, location data privacy, user data handling, AI-generated content disclaimers, and content moderation liability. Ensure the app operates within legal boundaries across target markets while maintaining user trust through transparent data practices.

## Scope Within ONO
- **Right of Publicity**: Celebrity/role model likeness usage in content and UI
- **Data Privacy**: GDPR, CCPA, and emerging privacy regulations compliance
- **Location Data**: If/when location is collected, storage and processing rules
- **User Data Handling**: Collection, processing, retention, and deletion policies
- **AI Disclaimers**: Labeling AI-generated content, advice disclaimers, liability limits
- **Content Moderation Liability**: Platform vs. publisher considerations, safe harbor provisions
- **Terms of Service & Privacy Policy**: User-facing legal documents

## Quality Standards
1. Privacy policy must be readable at Flesch-Kincaid Grade 8 level — legal accuracy without legal jargon
2. Every data collection point must have a documented legal basis (consent, legitimate interest, contract)
3. AI-generated content must always be labeled — no exceptions
4. Celebrity/role model content must have documented fair use analysis or licensing agreement
5. Data retention periods must be defined for every data category, enforced automatically
6. User consent must be granular, freely given, and as easy to withdraw as to grant
7. Legal documents must be version-controlled with change summaries for users

## Review Checklist
- [ ] Is there a documented legal basis for every category of personal data collected?
- [ ] Can users export all their data in a machine-readable format (GDPR Art. 20)?
- [ ] Is the right to deletion implemented as hard delete across all systems including backups?
- [ ] Do role model features use only publicly available information with fair use documentation?
- [ ] Are AI disclaimers visible at the point of content consumption, not buried in settings?
- [ ] Does the content moderation system comply with DSA (EU) requirements if applicable?
- [ ] Are age verification mechanisms in place (18+ minimum)?
- [ ] Is consent for push notifications separate from consent for data processing?

## Content Guidelines
### Celebrity Likeness & Right of Publicity
- **Safe Use**: Publicly available biographical facts, quotes with attribution, news coverage references
- **Risky Use**: Photos (even publicly available ones), stylized illustrations, voice/video clips
- **Prohibited**: Implying endorsement, creating fictional quotes, deepfakes or AI-generated likenesses
- **Per-Jurisdiction Variation**: Right of publicity varies by state (US) and country — document each
- **Mitigation Strategy**:
  - Use text-based role model profiles, not images, as the default
  - If images are used, license through proper stock/editorial photo agencies
  - Include disclaimer: "ONO is not affiliated with or endorsed by [person]"
  - Maintain a takedown response process — remove within 24 hours of valid complaint

### GDPR Compliance
- **Lawful Basis**: Consent for optional features, contract for core app functionality, legitimate interest for analytics (with balancing test documented)
- **Data Minimization**: Collect only what each feature requires — audit quarterly
- **Purpose Limitation**: Data collected for one purpose must not be repurposed without new consent
- **Storage Limitation**: Defined retention periods enforced automatically:
  - Account data: Duration of account + 30 days post-deletion
  - Progress data: Duration of account (exported on request)
  - Analytics: Anonymized after 30 days, deleted after 12 months
  - Progress photos: Auto-deleted after 90 days unless user opts for longer retention
  - AI interaction logs: 30 days for quality improvement, then deleted
- **Data Subject Rights**: Access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection — all implementable within 30 days
- **DPO**: Designate a Data Protection Officer when required by processing volume

### Location Data Privacy
- **Current Stance**: ONO should avoid collecting location data unless a specific feature requires it
- **If Required**: Collect only when feature is active (not background), at minimum precision needed
- **Storage**: Never store precise GPS coordinates — aggregate to city/region level if needed for analytics
- **Consent**: Separate, specific consent for location — never bundled with general data consent
- **Alternatives**: Timezone-based localization instead of GPS where possible

### AI-Generated Content Disclaimers
- **Labeling**: Every AI-generated response must display a visible indicator (icon + text)
- **Disclaimer Text**: "This content is generated by AI and is for educational purposes only. It is not professional advice."
- **Domain-Specific Disclaimers**:
  - Health content: "Not a substitute for medical advice. Consult a healthcare provider."
  - Financial content: "General education only. Not personalized financial advice."
  - Mental health: "Not therapy. If you're in crisis, contact [local emergency number]."
- **Liability Limitation**: Terms of Service must clearly limit liability for AI-generated content
- **User Awareness**: Onboarding must include AI content explanation and consent

### Content Moderation Liability
- **Platform Classification**: ONO operates as a platform, not a publisher — document this position
- **Safe Harbor**: Maintain compliance with Section 230 (US), DSA (EU), and equivalent local laws
- **Notice and Takedown**: Documented process for responding to legal content removal requests
- **Reporting**: Annual transparency report on content moderation actions (volume, categories, outcomes)
- **Record Keeping**: Retain moderation decisions and reasoning for 3 years for legal defense
- **Illegal Content**: Immediate removal protocol with law enforcement notification for CSAM, terrorism, imminent threats

### User Data Handling
- **Encryption**: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
- **Access Control**: Role-based access, principle of least privilege, access logging
- **Sub-Processors**: Documented list of all third-party processors (Supabase, Claude API, FCM)
- **Data Processing Agreements**: DPAs in place with all sub-processors before data flows
- **Breach Response**: 72-hour notification to supervisory authority (GDPR), user notification if high risk
- **International Transfers**: Standard Contractual Clauses for EU-US data transfers

## Red Flags to Avoid
- **Dark patterns in consent**: Pre-checked boxes, confusing double negatives, "consent walls"
- **Implied celebrity endorsement**: Any content that could be read as the person endorsing ONO
- **Invisible data collection**: Tracking behavior without clear disclosure
- **AI advice without disclaimers**: Especially in health, finance, and mental health domains
- **Undocumented data sharing**: Every third-party data flow must be documented and disclosed
- **Retention without limits**: Every data category must have a defined, enforced deletion timeline
- **Age verification gaps**: The app is 18+ — enforcement must be more than a checkbox
