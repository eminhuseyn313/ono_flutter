# [FINANCE] Expert Domain: Financial Literacy

## Purpose
Guide all financial content within ONO's Skill lane — budgeting fundamentals, investing basics, debt management, and earning potential. Ensure content is age-appropriate for 18-30 males, avoids harmful "get rich quick" mentality, and builds genuine financial capability through actionable micro-lessons. Financial content must empower without promoting reckless risk-taking.

## Scope Within ONO
- **Skill Lane (Finance Track)**: Budgeting actions, saving habits, investing micro-lessons
- **Financial Challenges**: Weekly money actions (track spending, automate savings, etc.)
- **Micro-Course Content**: 5-10 minute lessons on financial topics
- **Cross-Lane Integration**: Financial stress impact on Mind lane, career skills in Skill lane
- **Role Model Content**: Financial journeys of relatable figures (not billionaire worship)

## Quality Standards
1. All financial advice must be general education, never personalized financial advice (regulatory compliance)
2. Content must include "this is not financial advice" disclaimers where legally required
3. Investing content must present risk alongside return — never one without the other
4. No promotion of specific financial products, platforms, or instruments
5. Budgeting approaches must work for varying income levels, including low/unstable income
6. Debt content must be compassionate — no shame-based framing of financial mistakes
7. All dollar examples must scale to different currencies and cost-of-living contexts

- [ ] Does the content avoid recommending specific stocks, crypto tokens, or financial products?

## Content Guidelines
### Budgeting Actions
- Start with tracking, not restricting — "know where your money goes" before "cut spending"
- Teach multiple frameworks: 50/30/20, zero-based, envelope method — let users choose
- Weekly challenges: "Track every purchase this week" → "Identify your top 3 unnecessary expenses" → "Automate one savings transfer"
- Avoid: Extreme frugality culture, "latte factor" shaming, lifestyle comparison

### Investing Micro-Lessons
- **Foundation (Week 1-4)**: What is investing, compound interest, inflation, why starting early matters
- **Basics (Week 5-8)**: Index funds, diversification, risk tolerance, emergency fund first
- **Intermediate (Week 9-12)**: Tax-advantaged accounts (concept, not specific products), dollar-cost averaging
- **Advanced (Week 13+)**: Asset allocation, rebalancing, understanding market cycles
- Always emphasize: "Invest only what you can afford to lose" for anything beyond index funds
- Never include: Day trading, options, leveraged products, specific crypto recommendations

### Earning & Career
- Side income ideas grounded in skill-building, not "hustle culture"
- Salary negotiation scripts and practice scenarios
- Career planning tied to Skill lane progression
- Entrepreneurship basics — validation before investment, lean methodology

### Debt Management
- Normalize having debt — most young adults do
- Teach snowball vs. avalanche methods without dogma
- Student loan context (varies by country — present frameworks, not prescriptions)
- Credit score education: what it is, why it matters, how to build it responsibly
- Predatory lending awareness: payday loans, high-interest credit, BNPL traps

### Financial Wellness Integration
- Connect financial stress to Mind lane (anxiety, sleep, decision fatigue)
- Financial goal-setting as part of broader life vision
- "Enough" philosophy — defining personal financial sufficiency vs. endless accumulation

## Red Flags to Avoid
- **Get-rich-quick content**: Crypto moonshots, dropshipping promises, passive income fantasies
- **Guru worship**: "Follow this millionaire's morning routine and you'll be rich"
- **Shame-based framing**: "If you're broke at 25, it's your fault"
- **Product promotion**: Any content that reads as advertising for a financial service
- **Oversimplification**: "Just invest in the S&P 500" without discussing risk, timeline, or context
- **Class blindness**: Advice that only works with significant disposable income
- **Regulatory violation**: Anything that could be construed as personalized financial advice
