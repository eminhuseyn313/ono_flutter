# Hard days: when the plan can't happen

Emin's observation: there are days a person can't follow the rules — sick, swamped, a family emergency. A plan that ignores them breaks people. This note sets out what the research says and what ONO does about it. It applies to anyone, not one job.

## What the evidence says

| Finding | Source | What it means for ONO |
|---|---|---|
| A demanding goal **with a small emergency reserve** (e.g. 7 days a week with 2 emergency skips) was kept more often than an easy goal or a rigid one. People who used a reserve after a miss were more likely to keep going; the "emergency" label made them reluctant to use it casually. | Sharif & Shu, *Nudging persistence after failure through emergency reserves*, OBHDP 2019 (step-challenge field study + 4 lab studies) | A few labelled **emergency skips** per month, no rollover. A skip keeps the streak alive. |
| Missing **one** opportunity did not materially affect habit formation; people who were inconsistent overall did not form habits. | Lally et al., *European Journal of Social Psychology*, 2010 | One bad day is fine; a pattern isn't. Keep "never miss twice"; make the return easy. |
| A lapse turns into a relapse through the **abstinence-violation / "what-the-hell" effect**: one slip is read as proof of failure, and people give up. | Marlatt & Gordon 1985; Polivy & Herman (dieting) | The first day back must feel easy and the language must never shame. |
| The smallest version of a behaviour keeps the identity and the cue–routine link alive when motivation or capacity is low. | Fogg, *Tiny Habits* (2019); Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006 (if–then plans) | Every daily item has a **minimum version**. |
| Self-compassion after a setback increases the motivation to improve. | Breines & Chen 2012 | Copy: "part of any plan worth keeping", "your streak waits for you". |

## What ONO does

| Kind | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum day** | Swamped, low, travelling | The smallest version of each item counts; half the items (rounded up) holds the day. Unlimited — a held minimum day is still a held day. |
| **Sick day** | Ill | Nothing expected; neutral for the streak; excluded from item rates. From day 3 in a row: "if it isn't getting better, see a doctor". No other medical advice. |
| **Emergency skip** | Something you couldn't plan for | Neutral for the streak. 2 per program month, no rollover. |
| **Planned rest** (existing) | Rest days, holidays | Neutral, as before. |
| **Welcome back** (automatic) | First day after a sick day, or after 2+ days off | A minimum day automatically: the return is easy, so a lapse doesn't become a relapse. |

- Choose it on Today ("Hard day? Choose how today counts"), for today or yesterday (the 48-hour window). Undo anytime.
- Minimum versions are edited on If-then plans (`/plans`), next to each cue. General library items and plan packs carry their own.
- The Sunday brief counts hard days by kind; after 2+ sick days it suggests returning on the minimum for two days; after 3+ minimum days or skips it suggests changing the plan, not the person's opinion of themselves.

## Open questions for later
